The Government of Canada's [digital sovereignty framework](https://www.canada.ca/en/government/system/digital-government/digital-government-innovations/cloud-services/digital-sovereignty/digital-sovereignty-framework-improve-digital-readiness.html)
frames control, assurance and resilience as goals.
[Federal guidance](https://www.canada.ca/en/government/system/digital-government/guideline-service-digital.html#ToC4_4)
calls for evaluating Canadian computing facilities or specified government
premises abroad as a principal delivery option for Protected B, Protected C
and classified information; its residency rule concerns stored data, not transit.
[Nova Scotia's law](https://nslegislature.ca/sites/default/files/legc/statutes/persinfo.htm)
requires Canadian storage and access for public-body personal information,
subject to section 5 exceptions.
[British Columbia's Act](https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96165_03)
regulates public-body disclosure of personal information outside Canada.

This showcase demonstrates three configurable application controls that support those sovereignty objectives; it does not certify an application or establish complete sovereignty.

## Showcase objectives

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Host the application in Canada">
    Place the application origin in Canada and discover it through the owned Canadian Customer Edges. This objective demonstrates where the origin runs and how the application selects it for incoming requests.

    - [Explore Canadian origin configuration](./use-case/#origin-pool--canadian-origin)
    - [Map the origin to configuration](./architecture/)
  </Card>
  <Card title="Serve through Canadian Regional Edges">
    Bind a reserved public allocation to Toronto and Montreal Regional Edges, then advertise the application listener through that allocation. This objective demonstrates the intended Canadian public entry points for the application.

    - [Explore regional advertisement](./use-case/#load-balancer--regional-advertisement)
    - [Verify Regional Edge placement](./verification/)
  </Card>
  <Card title="Control access by source country">
    Apply a policy to the actual client source: permit sources classified as Canadian and deny other or unknown classifications. This objective demonstrates country-based authorization without trusting a client-supplied forwarding header.

    - [Explore client authorization](./use-case/#load-balancer--client-authorization)
    - [Follow the proof sequence](./presentation/)
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

Canadian entry-point placement does not establish end-to-end domestic packet routing. Source-country classification does not establish a user's identity or physical location.
