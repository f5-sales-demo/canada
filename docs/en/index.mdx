## Canadian hosting and regional access control

Follow an application request from tenant-managed DNS to a Canadian public listener, an actual-source country policy, and an origin discovered through Canadian Customer Edges. The [use case](./use-case/) explains each control with API configuration excerpts for existing F5 Distributed Cloud users.

The demonstration places three Customer Edges and its origin in Canada Central and binds a reserved public allocation to Toronto/Montreal Regional Edges. A selected service policy permits `COUNTRY_CA` and denies other or unknown classifications, with forwarding-header trust disabled.

Read the [test reference](./verification/) to understand the observations, or consult the [configuration mapping](./architecture/) and [Terraform sources](./terraform/) when adapting the pattern. Staging, recovery, and lifecycle details remain in the reference navigation.

Canadian origin placement, regional advertisement, and source-country authorization are independent decisions. The demonstrated node recovery is within Canada Central; it does not establish cross-region redundancy, comprehensive data residency, or legal compliance.
